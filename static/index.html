01 / Frame the evaluation

Inspect exported UI screenshots using a framework mapped to Nielsen’s usability heuristics. This prototype does not interact with your interface.

1–3 images · up to 2 MB and 12 megapixels each. Export from your design tool and remove personal or confidential information.

Live review makes three sequential model calls and may take up to six minutes. Azure charges may apply. Keys stay on the local server.

02 / Inspect and decide

Review each observation against the screenshot. Confirm, reject or mark it for further investigation; record your reasoning.

Your evaluation will appear here.
Start with the demo to explore the review workflow.

A review framework, not a verdict

Screenshot inspection cannot establish keyboard access, screen-reader support, response times or real-world task success. There is no automated compliance certificate or aggregate usability score. AI findings are provisional; a lack of visible issues is not a pass.

Framework: Jakob Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics. Short rubric descriptions in this project are paraphrases. Independent project; no NN/g or Microsoft endorsement.
